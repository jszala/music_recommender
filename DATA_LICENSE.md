# Data and license status

Public recording metadata comes from MusicBrainz. Its core artist, recording,
release, relationship, and work data is [CC0](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/About/Data_License).
Supplementary tags/ratings have different terms and are not included. Source
requirements were checked on 2026-10-08.

- [Saved recommendations](data/recommendations/README.md): 19 unmodified snapshots
  supporting all 11 results and eight matched favorites in their credit paths,
  collected on 2026-10-08.
- [Constructed fixture](data/demo/README.md): 39 unmodified snapshots from 2026-10-07.
- [Historical graph fixture](data/fixture/README.md): four snapshots from 2026-10-06.

Each manifest records source URLs, exact queries, retrieval dates, and SHA-256
checksums. The saved output reports have their own checksum binding. Full personal
input, unused sampled likes, private ratings, contact details, and request caches
are excluded. Only matched recording context needed to explain the published
recommendations is included.

No cover art or audio is bundled; missing artwork is omitted. Apple links are
labeled DE-region searches rather than verified track matches and do not guarantee
availability or playback. They do not affect ranking.

No code license has been applied. MIT is proposed, pending the author's selection
and copyright attribution. Metadata retains its separate licensing status.
