# First public version: v0.1 candidate

An offline music-credit recommendation experiment. The README displays all 11
recordings from three saved runs in their original order, with short credit paths
and source links. The method, full evidence, and experiment results are linked
separately. No standalone page, screenshot, or missing-art placeholder is included.

The runs returned 4/10, 3/10, and 4/10; the livestream and continuous mix remain
visible. Scores, weights, exclusions, and historical results are unchanged. The
small constructed fixture still reproduces scoring and selection offline.

MusicBrainz core metadata is CC0. Private inputs, contacts, ratings, audio, and
artwork are excluded. MIT is proposed for code; attribution/selection remain pending.
See [verification](docs/verification.md) and [the inventory](PUBLIC_FILES.txt).
